  • Each transaction opens with the rules that fired and whether it went to the manual queue.

03 HOW IT WORKS

Risky withdrawals
wait for a person.

When a withdrawal's score reaches the risk limit you set, Sentinel puts it in a manual queue. Your team reviews it with the rules that fired and labels the outcome.

  • Risk score

    Each event gets a risk level and score from the rules that fired.

  • When to alert

    The triggers you set decide which events raise an alert.

  • Risk limit

    A withdrawal whose score reaches your limit waits in the manual queue.

  • Review steps

    Each alert opens with the event data and the steps to review it.

  • Fraud or legitimate

    After review, your team labels the outcome.

  • Activity log

    What each teammate opened and did, and when.

04 ACCESS & INTEGRATION

Access by role.
Activity on record.

Your team works through roles built from a permission matrix, and the activity log keeps what each person did. Your platform connects through API keys, webhooks and field mapping.

  • Roles and permissions

    Each role is a set of permissions in a matrix; teammates get access through their role.

  • Activity log

    Sign-ins, the screens and customer files opened, and the actions taken.

  • Sign-in settings

    How your team signs in to Sentinel.

  • API keys

    Keys your platform uses to send events to Sentinel.

  • Webhooks

    Sentinel notifies your systems of results.

  • Field mapping

    Your platform's fields matched to Sentinel's.

06 THE PAM BACK-OFFICEIn development

Next: player accounts,
payments and compliance.

RHS Labs is building the player account management back-office that completes Sentinel. These areas are in development and not yet available.

Features and timing are confirmed in commercial discussions.

  • Player accounts

    Player search with combinable criteria, and Player 360: summary, KYC, payments, wallet, risk, limits, games and history.

  • Payments

    A withdrawal queue with approval limits by role and player category, cashier limits, unmatched payments and balance batches.

  • Compliance

    An audit report with an integrity check, responsible gaming, a KYC queue, data-subject requests and policy versions.

  • Engagement

    Segments, bonus campaigns, bulk actions, a VIP portfolio and service tickets.

  • Reports

    The operator's financial summary, margins, negative balances, active blocks, payout times and job health.

  • Administration

    Staff roles built from granular permissions, approval limits, tags and handling-time targets.
